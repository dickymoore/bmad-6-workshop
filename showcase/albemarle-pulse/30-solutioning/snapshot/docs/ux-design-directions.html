UX Design Directions

Six visual directions for a calm, fact-only, Royal Institution foyer display. Each direction keeps the same product contract and data grammar, but shifts hierarchy, map emphasis, and visual weight to test how the screen should feel in the room.

The references baked into these directions are: Apple Weather for atmospheric first read, Flighty for trustworthy status hierarchy, and TfL signage for public readability and shared orientation.

How to evaluate Look for the direction that makes the room-scale read obvious, keeps trust cues composed, and feels most building-native rather than app-like.
Core test Could a visitor understand “calm, strained, or disrupted” in 2-3 seconds and then confirm the story in 5-10 seconds up close?
Calm / viable Strained / caution Disrupted / reduced trust
