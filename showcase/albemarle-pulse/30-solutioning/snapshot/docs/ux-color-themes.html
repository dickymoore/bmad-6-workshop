Albemarle Pulse Theme Studies

This visualizer explores a small set of calm, civic, display-first palettes for Albemarle Pulse. Each option keeps the same product grammar: light architectural neutrals, charcoal structure, restrained accent metals or minerals, and muted green/amber/red status states.

The goal is not brand variety for its own sake. It is to test how the foyer display can feel building-native, trustworthy, and legible at distance without looking like a dashboard or consumer app.

Use this alongside the design directions. A strong default would likely be `Civic Light` or `Quiet Parchment`, depending on how much warmth versus institutional clarity you want in the foyer.

  • Large display type remains primary in every theme.
  • Status colors stay muted and sophisticated.
  • Atmospheric tone shifts should stay subtle, never theatrical.
